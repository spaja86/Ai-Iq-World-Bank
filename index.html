INDEKURILANC

INDEKURILANC is the active repository scoring baseline for AI capability maturity. The current standard keeps three weighted dimensions and adds an operational interpretation.

Baseline Standard

  • Infrastructure: 40%
  • Skills: 35%
  • Governance: 25%
  • Active reference: INDEKURILANC-STD-V1

Use the score as a structured readiness indicator, not as a substitute for a full business, governance, or licensing review.
