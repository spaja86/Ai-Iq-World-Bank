                1. Foundation stabilization: structure, hierarchy, standards, and validation.
                2. Product strengthening: clearer prototype surfaces, score breakdowns, and standard-aligned outputs.
                3. Portfolio alignment: roadmap, operating model, ownership, and document metadata.
