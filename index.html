    INDEKURILANC is the active repository scoring baseline for AI capability maturity. The current standard keeps three weighted dimensions and requires an operational interpretation.

    • Skills: 35%
    • Governance: 25%

    Use the score as a structured readiness indicator, not as a substitute for a full business, governance, or licensing review.
